Council Rock North Bands

Why Music?

The Council Rock North Bands program gives students the opportunity to grow not only as musicians, but as people, building community, teamwork, dedication, musicianship, and other life-long skills all while partaking in fun performances and events.

Students in the program develop strong musical skills, leadership, and friendships that last a lifetime.

Contact the Band Program

Our Ensembles

Concert Band

Concert Band focuses on musical growth and performance skills through classes, rehearsal, and concerts.

Indoor Drumline

Indoor drumline consists of only percussion instruments in a marching-band-style show performed indoors.

Indoor Color Guard

Indoor Color Guard consists of a visual and dance show performed indoors.

Marching Band

Marching Band prepares a field show and stand tunes, performing in football games, parades, and competitions over the fall season.

Jazz Band

Jazz Band (split into Jazz I and Jazz II) focuses on advanced technique, style, improvisation, and ensemble musicianship through competitions and community performances.
